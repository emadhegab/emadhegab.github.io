Storiapod

ستوريا بود · Arabic audio stories and drama

Storiapod grew out of a long-standing friendship. Kareem Kandil, founder of Storia Pod GmbH, had envisioned building his own app for years.

Once the right technology and the right motivation came together, I committed fully to turning that vision into a product, and I lead its engineering end to end: native iOS and Android apps on a shared Kotlin Multiplatform core, backed by a Rails platform.

Following a successful launch, Storiapod now serves 10,000 users and growing, with one mission: not only to tell the story, but to put the listener inside it.
